## About me

I'm a product-minded developer from Bangladesh who enjoys turning complex ideas into clear, useful, and maintainable software. My journey began with front-end development, especially React, and has grown into building complete applications across the interface, server, data, authentication, and deployment layers.

I am especially interested in AI-enabled products, real-time applications, operational dashboards, and multi-step business workflows. I care about the details people actually feel: readable interfaces, responsive layouts, sensible loading and error states, secure role-based access, and smooth handoffs between users.

- 🔭 I build full-stack web applications, AI tools, dashboards, and workflow systems.
- 🧠 I work on RAG experiences, voice and email integrations, role-based portals, and real-time data.
- 🎨 I enjoy translating complicated requirements into simple, user-friendly interfaces.
- 🛠️ I value maintainable TypeScript, reusable components, thoughtful data models, and practical testing.
- 🌱 I keep learning about AI engineering, system design, performance, and reliable product delivery.
- 🤝 I enjoy collaborating on products that solve real problems for real people.
